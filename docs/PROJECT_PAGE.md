## Title
Earth Jukebox - hear NASA's Earth Information Center

## One-line summary
Earth Jukebox turns NASA Earth Information Center maps (night lights, sea surface temperature, lightning) into sound, graphs, tables and a 3D coil, so people who cannot see the maps can still hear how our planet changes.

## What it does
- Choose one of three NASA datasets, then upload a video or any number of JPG, PNG, TIFF or PDF pictures.
- A yellow scan line reads the picture from left to right. Colors and brightness become pitch, rhythm and stereo position; a voice explains every change.
- Graph, accessible table, 3D coil, plain-language conclusions, and a country/continent panel (night lights, lightning) or ocean-basin panel (sea surface temperature).
- Each dataset keeps its own settings, so the three are never mixed.
- Accessibility: full keyboard control, voice guide, high contrast, larger text, explore mode, screen-reader announcements. Everything is in English.

## How we used NASA data
Sample frames and reading rules come from NASA SVS / EIC visualizations: Black Marble night lights, MIT/JPL ECCO2 sea surface temperature and currents, and ISS Lightning Imaging Sensor lightning. Values are image-based estimates, not official NASA measurements.
